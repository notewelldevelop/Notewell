# Install NoteWell on your iPad

**Five steps, about two minutes, free. You need Safari and nothing else.**

---

### 1. Open the link in Safari

> **`https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/notewell/`**

It has to be **Safari**. Chrome on an iPad can show NoteWell but Apple doesn't
let it install apps.

### 2. Tap the Share button

The square with an arrow coming out of the top, in Safari's toolbar.

### 3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the list — it's below the row of apps. The name and the NW icon
fill themselves in.

### 4. Tap "Add"

Top right. Now close Safari completely.

### 5. Open NoteWell from your home screen, once, with wifi on

Give it about ten seconds. It's copying itself onto your iPad.

**That's it.** From now on it opens like any other app and works with no
connection at all.

---

### Check it worked

Turn on Aeroplane Mode and open NoteWell. If you can write, you're done.

If it doesn't open, go back to Safari, load the link again, wait a bit longer,
and add it to your home screen again.

---

## Updates

You don't have to do anything. NoteWell checks for a new version when you open
it, and a small bar appears at the bottom saying one is ready. Tap **Update
now** and it takes a second.

**Your notes are never affected by an update.** They live in your iPad's own
storage, not in the app files.

If you'd rather check yourself: **⚙ Settings → Check for updates**. The version
you're on is listed right above it.

## Using it with an Apple Pencil

Pair the Pencil in Settings → Apple Pencil, then:

- **The Pencil draws, fingers scroll.** Rest your whole hand on the screen —
  a palm can't draw, move the page, or interrupt a stroke. No Pencil? Turn on
  **Settings → Writing → Finger draws**.
- **Press harder** for a thicker line. **Lay the Pencil over** to shade with
  the side of the nib.
- **Double-tap the Pencil** to swap between pen and eraser. In the web version
  that's two quick taps of the tip on the screen, not the squeeze on the barrel
  — Apple doesn't let web apps hear the barrel gesture. A mis-tap never leaves
  a mark.
- **Scribble back and forth** over a mistake and it disappears, scribble and all.
- **Draw a rough box or circle and hold still** for half a second — it snaps.
- **Keep pulling past the bottom** of the last page and hold to add a page.

## Where your notes live, and keeping them safe

Your notes are saved on your iPad the instant you write them. No connection
needed, ever.

**Two ways to have a second copy:**

**A file backup** — the shelf's **Backup / restore** → **Save to Files**. Choose
iCloud Drive and it's on all your devices. Worth doing every week or two; the
app will remind you if it's been a while.

**An account** — if you've been given a NoteWell server address, sign in under
the person icon. After that it saves by itself: when you open a notebook, about
twenty seconds after you stop writing, and when you close it.

**With no wifi**, the sync chip in the toolbar says *"Offline — saved on this
iPad"*. That's not an error. Everything you write is already saved locally, and
it uploads itself the moment you're back online. You never have to remember to
press anything.

If you use NoteWell on an iPad *and* a laptop, both sync to the same account and
NoteWell merges them notebook by notebook — whichever copy of each notebook you
edited most recently is the one that's kept. Two devices working on two
different notebooks both keep their work.

## If something goes wrong

Tap **Install** in the top bar first. It tells you whether you're installed,
whether the address is secure, and whether the offline copy is in place. That
usually answers it.

**"Add to Home Screen" isn't in the Share menu**
You're not in Safari, or you're in a Private tab. Use a normal Safari tab.

**The page looks like plain unstyled text**
Something didn't load. Pull down to refresh, or force-close and reopen.

**The icon is a grey page instead of the NW monogram**
Safari cached the old one. Remove the home-screen icon, reload the link in
Safari, and add it again.

**Nothing happens when I write with the Pencil**
Check you're on the Pen tool. If a finger works but the Pencil doesn't,
force-close NoteWell and reopen it — the Pencil wasn't connected when the page
loaded.

**My palm draws**
It shouldn't. Settings → Writing → make sure **Finger draws** is unticked.

**My notes vanished**
Almost always a different address. `github.io/notewell/` and
`github.io/notewell` (no slash) are the same, but a different repo or username
is a completely separate NoteWell with its own storage. Check the link matches
the one you installed from.

This is exactly why the backup file matters — make one now if you haven't.

**It says a new version is available every time I open it**
Tap Update now once and let it finish. If it keeps asking, force-close the app
and reopen.
